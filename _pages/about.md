I am R&D engineer at [EDF](https://www.edf.fr/), the main french public electricity company. I work in department SEQUOIA on modeling electricity consumption and demand. Please feel free to reach out!

Previously, I worked at [Groupe SNCF](https://www.groupe-sncf.com/), the main french public railway company, in department eSNCF on ML applications to railway-related problems. Before that, I did a postdoc at the [Sierra](https://www.di.ens.fr/sierra/) Inria research team. I worked with [Alessandro Rudi](https://www.di.ens.fr/~rudi/) on kernel methods and Sum of Squares models, with applications to statistics and machine learning. I did my PhD at [Aramis Lab](https://www.aramislab.fr/), an Inria research team at the Brain and Spine Institute, and [CMAP](https://portail.polytechnique.edu/cmap/en/), the applied mathematics lab of École polytechnique. I worked under the direction of [Stéphanie Allassonnière](https://sites.google.com/site/stephanieallassonniere/) and [Stanley Durrleman](https://who.rocq.inria.fr/Stanley.Durrleman/) on data sets of brain networks and how to model them from a statistical perspective.
